# Harry Potter: Hogwarts Mystery Character Sheets

1.  Liam Reed
- **House:** Gryffindor
- **Year:** Gradually progress through the story
- **Role:** Protagonist, Curse-Breaker
- **Appearance:** Silver hair styled in a short mullet, blue eyes, 171 cm
- **Personality:** Courageous, reckless, quick-witted, determined, stubborn, loyal
- **Special Abilities:** Curse-breaking, vault investigation
- **Relationships:** 
  - Brother to Jacob (missing)
  - Close friends with the curse-breaker group
  - Trusts Tulip and Hayami with Jacob's research
- **Story Arc:** Leading the investigation into the cursed vaults to find his missing brother

2. Rowan Khanna
- **House:** Gryffindor
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** First friend, best friend, loyal companion
- **Appearance:** Shot black hair, blue eyes, with glasses, 170 cm
- **Personality:** Studious, loyal, bookish, eager to help, sometimes anxious
- **Special Abilities:** Extensive knowledge from reading, research skills
- **Relationships:** Liam's first and closest friend, roommate
- **Story Arc:** Constant companion throughout the curse-breaking adventures

3. Ben Copper
- **House:** Gryffindor
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Nervous friend who grows braver over time
- **Appearance:** Short sandy blonde hair, brown eyes, 169 cm
- **Personality:** Initially timid and fearful, gradually becomes braver, knowledgeable about dark magic
- **Special Abilities:** Surprisingly knowledgeable about dark magic and dangerous creatures
- **Relationships:** Close friend of Liam, often needs encouragement from the group
- **Story Arc:** Character growth from fearful to brave, sometimes recklessly so

4. Bill Weasley
- **House:** Gryffindor
- **Year:** Older student (starts in Year 3 when Liam is in Year 1)
- **Role:** Year 2 Introduction →, Mentor, curse-breaker expertise
- **Appearance:** Long red hair shoulder-length, tall, amber eyes, friendly demeanor, 180 cm
- **Personality:** Helpful, knowledgeable, protective, mature
- **Special Abilities:** Curse-breaking, advanced magic, leadership
- **Relationships:** Mentor figure to Liam and the group
- **Story Arc:** Guides the younger students, eventually graduates

5. Charlie Weasley
- **House:** Gryffindor
- **Year:** Same as Liam
- **Role:** Year 4 Introduction → Dragon expert, Quidditch player
- **Appearance:** Red hair, amber eyes, stocky build, friendly, 173 cm
- **Personality:** Adventurous, dragon-obsessed, brave, loyal
- **Special Abilities:** Dragon handling, Quidditch, magical creatures knowledge
- **Relationships:** Friend and ally to Liam, Bill's younger brother
- **Story Arc:** Provides dragon expertise when needed

6. Jae Kim
- **House:** Gryffindor
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Year 4 Introduction → Entrepreneur, rule-bender
- **Appearance:** Messy short black hair, brown eyes, 171 cm
- **Personality:** Entrepreneurial, clever, rule-bending, resourceful
- **Special Abilities:** Business sense, finding/acquiring items
- **Relationships:** Friend to Liam, often provides needed items or services
- **Story Arc:** Provides resources and alternative perspectives

7.  Penny Haywood
- **House:** Hufflepuff
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Popular, helpful friend and potions expert
- **Appearance:** Blonde hair styled in braids, blue eyes, friendly appearance, 165 cm
- **Personality:** Outgoing, popular, kind, helpful, social butterfly, nurturing
- **Special Abilities:** Excellent at potions, knows everyone at Hogwarts
- **Relationships:** 
  - Best friends with Mayumi (helped her come out of her shell)
  - Close friend to Liam and most of the group
  - Particularly fond of Mayumi
  - Loves to tease Tulip and Hayami about their close friendship
- **Story Arc:** Consistent supportive friend, often the social connector of the group

8.  Mayumi Sasaki
- **House:** Hufflepuff
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Year 1 side character → Year 3 major character
- **Appearance:** Long straight grey hair, blue eyes, 165 cm
- **Personality:** Meticulous, sweet, awkward, hesitant, intuitive, mellow, quiet
- **Special Abilities:** Expert in Ancient Runes, deciphering vault messages
- **Relationships:**
  - Best friends with Penny Haywood (who helped her come out of her shell)
  - Acts as the group's "mother figure"
  - Particularly fond relationship with Penny
- **Story Arc:** Shy runes expert introduced by Penny who becomes essential to vault investigations, taking on major role in Year 3

9. Nymphadora Tonks
- **House:** Hufflepuff
- **Year:** Same as Liam
- **Role:** Year 3 Introduction → Metamorphmagus, rule-bender
- **Appearance:** Changes frequently due to metamorphmagus abilities
- **Personality:** Fun-loving, rule-bending, energetic, supportive
- **Special Abilities:** Metamorphmagus powers, auror training
- **Relationships:** Ally to Liam, enjoys causing harmless chaos
- **Story Arc:** Provides unique magical abilities and perspective

10. Chiara Lobosca
- **House:** Hufflepuff
- **Year:** Same as Liam
- **Role:** Year 3 Introduction → Werewolf
- **Appearance:** White hair styled in bob, yellow eyes, 163 cm
- **Personality:** Supportive, wary, individual, nurturing, anxious
- **Special Abilities:** Werewolf and healing expert
- **Relationships:** 
 - Friend to Liam, although she pushed him away at first
 - Specially close with Mayumi
 - Mayumi was the first one to know about her being a werewolf
- **Story Arc:** Provides healing need for the group

11. Tulip Karasu
- **House:** Ravenclaw
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Year 3 Introduction →  Mischievous prankster, Animagus (Angora cat)
- **Appearance:** Red hair styled in a shoulder-length butterfly cut, hazel eyes, 170 cm
- **Personality:** Mischievous, clever, rebellious, independent, guarded (especially after Merula fallout), creative, intellectual 
- **Special Abilities:** Pranks, mischief, sneaking around, lock-picking, tranfiguration
- **Relationships:**
  - Roommates and best friends with Hayami
  - Former friends with Merula (fell out in Year 2)
  - Became guarded after Merula fallout, but opened up to Hayami
- **Story Arc:** Recovering from friendship betrayal, finding new trust with Hayami

12. Hayami Amamiya
- **House:** Ravenclaw
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Year 4 proper introduction → Year 5 major character, Animagus (American Shepherd dog)
- **Appearance:** Short blonde hair styled in a wolf cut, green eyes, 173 cm
- **Personality:** Analytical, charismatic, resourceful, assertive, aloof, cynical, instinctive, vigilant
- **Special Abilities:** Expert duelist, combat training for the group
- **Relationships:**
  - Roommates and best friends with Tulip Karasu
  - The group's duelling tutor
  - Gets dragged into Tulip's schemes despite preferring peace
- **Story Arc:** Reluctantly joins vault investigations after dramatic rescue in Forbidden Forest, becomes group's dueling tutor

13. Talbott Winger
- **House:** Ravenclaw
- **Year:** Same as Liam
- **Role:** Year 4 Introduction → Animagus (red-tailed hawk)
- **Appearance:** Slick back brown short hair, brown eyes, 175 cm
- **Personality:** Reserved, closed-off, quiet, meticulous, organized
- **Special Abilities:** Information gathering, sneeking
- **Relationships:** 
  - A bit wary of Liam at first
  - Actually has a pretty close bond with Andre, Hayami, And Tulip
  - After being scared to register himself to the minister as an animagus, three of his close friends agree to be animagus too so he wouldn't feel lonely
- **Story Arc:** After so many pestering from Liam, he finally agreed to be friends, even though still denied it

14. Andre Egwu
- **House:** Ravenclaw
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Year 2 Introduction → Fashion enthusiast, Social butterfly, Animagus (Thuringer rabbit)
- **Appearance:** Buzz cut, brown eyes, Well-dressed, fashion-conscious, 171 cm
- **Personality:** Fashion-focused, social, enthusiastic about style
- **Special Abilities:** Fashion knowledge, social connections
- **Relationships:** Friend to Liam, social butterfly
- **Story Arc:** Provides social connections and fashion advice

15. Barnaby Lee
- **House:** Slytherin
- **Year:**  Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Year 3 Introduction → Strong but initially not very bright, becomes loyal friend
- **Appearance:** Short brown hair, green eyes, tall, muscular, intimidating but friendly face, 176 cm
- **Personality:** Physically strong, initially naive, loyal once befriended, good-hearted despite Slytherin stereotypes
- **Special Abilities:** Physical strength, gradually improving magical abilities
- **Relationships:** Close friend to Liam, accepted by the group despite initial suspicions
- **Story Arc:** Growth from Merula's follower to independent friend of the group

16. Merula Snyde
- **House:** Slytherin
- **Year:** Same year as Liam
- **Role:** Primary rival, later complicated ally, can be considered the antagonist force
- **Appearance:** Brown hair styled in a short layered cut, brown eyes, 167 cm
- **Personality:** Ambitious, prideful, competitive, initially antagonistic, complex motivations
- **Special Abilities:** Skilled at magic, particularly dark magic knowledge
- **Relationships:**
  - Primary rival to Liam
  - Former friends with Tulip (fell out in Year 2)
  - Complicated relationship with the group
- **Story Arc:** Evolution from pure antagonist to complex character with her own motivations
