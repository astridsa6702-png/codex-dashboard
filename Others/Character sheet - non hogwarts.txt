Character Sheets

1.  Liam Reed  (he/they)
- **Role:** The Explorer – Bold, curious, and always chasing the next experience. Loves trying new things and dragging the group into unexpected adventures.
- **Appearance:** Silver hair styled in a short mullet, blue eyes, 171 cm
- **Personality:** Adventurous, curios, inspiring, impulsive, restless, Spontaneous, Independent
- **Special Abilities:** Fire – Classic and chaotic. Burn it all or warm your hands. Your choice.
- **Relationships:** 
  - Close friends with the gang
  - Rival to Merula
  - Best friend to Rowan
- **Story Arc:** Learns that chasing every thrill means nothing without people worth coming home to.

2. Rowan Khanna  (they/them)
- **Role:** The Brain – Smart and resourceful, always has a plan or solution.
- **Appearance:** Shot black hair, blue eyes, with glasses, 170 cm
- **Personality:** Analytical, Knowledgeable, Dependable, Know-It-All, detached, Perfectionist, reserved
- **Special Abilities:** Sound – Sonic booms, vibration waves, and the power to shatter eardrums and glass.
- **Relationships:** Liam's first and closest friend, roommate
- **Story Arc:** Realizes that not every problem can be solved with logic—some require heart.

3. Ben Copper  (he/they)
- **Role:** The Overthinker – Constantly analyzing situations, sometimes worries too much but sees hidden angles.
- **Appearance:** Short sandy blonde hair, brown eyes, 169 cm
- **Personality:** Insightful, cautious, reflective, indecisive, anxious, Self-Critical, introspective
- **Special Abilities:** Earth – Grounded power. Boulders, armor, and earthquakes included.
- **Relationships:** Close friend of Liam, often needs encouragement from the group
- **Story Arc:** Learns to trust their instincts and that not every decision needs to be perfect to be right.

4. Jae Kim (he/him)

- **Appearance:** Messy short black hair, brown eyes, 171 cm
- **Personality:** Funny, optimistic, opportunistic, avoidant, Inappropriate, playful, Attention-Seeking
- **Special Abilities:** Wind/Air – Slicing gusts, flight, and casually flipping tables without lifting a finger.
- **Relationships:** Friend to Liam, often provides needed items or services
- **Story Arc:** Discovers that being the funny one doesn’t mean hiding their real feelings forever.

5.  Penny Haywood (she/her)

  - Particularly fond of Mayumi

  - Also loves to tease Andre and Talbott

6.  Mayumi Sasaki (she/her)

  - Best friends with Penny Haywood (who helped her come out of her shell)

  - Particularly fond relationship with Chiara

7. Chiara Lobosca (she/her)

- **Appearance:** White hair styled in bob, yellow eyes, 163 cm

8. Tulip Karasu (she/her)

- **Relationships:**
  - Roommates and best friends with Hayami

9. Hayami Amamiya (she/they)

- **Personality:** realistic, witty, loyal, distrustful, pessimistic, blunt, charismatic
- **Special Abilities:** Lightning – Fast, furious, shocking, and destructive. Zeus would approve.

  - Roommates and best friends with Tulip Karasu
