The Four Founders' Legacies

House Valerius

"Fortiter in Re" - Bold in Action

Founded by Valerius the Bold, this house values courage, action, and honor. Valerians are known for their bravery and unwavering sense of justice.

Colors: Gold and Red

House Seraphina

"Succurrere et Servare" - To Aid and To Protect

Founded by Seraphina the Compassionate, this house champions empathy, protection, and community. Seraphins are the heart of the Athenaeum.

Colors: Silver and Blue

House Orion

"Sapientia et Ars" - Wisdom and Artifice

Founded by Orion the Far-Sighted, this house treasures intellect, curiosity, and foresight. Orions are the scholars and tacticians.

Colors: Bronze and Green

House Morwenna

"Audere et Effingere" - To Dare and To Shape

Founded by Morwenna the Unchained, this house celebrates ambition, resourcefulness, and independence. Morwennans are innovators and individualists.

Colors: Iron and Purple

Excellence Through Tradition

For over five centuries, Lumina Athenaeum has stood as a beacon of academic excellence and character development. Our Gothic halls have witnessed the education of countless distinguished graduates who have gone on to serve in positions of great responsibility and honor.


Founded on the principles embodied in our motto "Ignis Mentis" - The Fire of the Mind - we believe that true strength comes from the disciplined cultivation of both intellectual prowess and moral character.


Our unique house system, established by our four legendary founders, ensures that every student finds their place within our community while learning the values essential for leadership in an complex world.

Campus Facilities

The Grand Athenaeum

Our main building houses administrative offices, classrooms, and the magnificent Great Hall where the entire academy gathers for meals and ceremonies.

The Quartz Archives

An extensive library and knowledge repository connected to the main building via a covered glass bridge, featuring rare texts and research materials.

The Aura Coliseum

State-of-the-art training facilities and competition venues, including specialized chambers for practical applications and skill development.

House Towers

Four distinct residential towers, each reflecting the unique character and traditions of their founding house, providing students with comfortable living spaces.

The Whispering Woods

Ancient forests surrounding the campus, used for outdoor education, survival training, and quiet contemplation among nature.

The Lake of Reflection

A serene body of water perfect for meditation and reflection, said to provide clarity to those who seek wisdom in its still depths.
