The Veil Accord

Acknowledging the existence of anomalous abilities within the human population, and recognizing the dual necessity of safeguarding humanity from potential threats while protecting the bearers of said abilities from persecution, the undersigned parties hereby establish this Accord to govern the existence and operations of the Lumina Athenaeum.

Article I: Sovereignty and Secrecy (The "Veil")

• Section 1: The Lumina Athenaeum and its grounds are recognized as a sovereign extraterritorial entity, independent of the laws of the host nation(s). In return, the Athenaeum must remain utterly concealed from the general public.

• Section 2: The existence of superhuman abilities, the Athenaeum, and all events related to them shall be classified under the highest level of state secrecy by all signatory governments. This body of knowledge is henceforth referred to as "The Veil."

• Section 3: The Athenaeum is responsible for maintaining its own secrecy through advanced concealment technologies and magical wards. Signatory governments will assist in this by creating exclusion zones (e.g., designating the area as a "radioactive hazard" or "nature reserve" on public maps).


Article II: Recruitment and Governance

• Section 1: The Athenaeum retains the sole right to identify, test, and recruit individuals with potential abilities ("Potentials").

• Section 2: A joint oversight committee, the "Veil Council," shall be formed. It will consist of three Athenaeum representatives (including the Headmaster) and three representatives from the signatory governments' intelligence communities.

• Section 3: The Veil Council's primary role is to ensure compliance with this Accord and to act as a liaison for joint operations under Article III.


Article III: Operational Protocol (The "Quid Pro Quo")

• Section 1: Threat Neutralization. The Athenaeum is obligated to assist in the containment or elimination of supernatural threats that exceed the capabilities of conventional forces. This includes rogue superhumans, hostile extradimensional entities, or dangerous magical artifacts.

• Section 2: Disaster Response. Graduates of the Athenaeum may be discreetly embedded into emergency response teams during natural or man-made disasters to minimize loss of life using their abilities, provided the Veil is not compromised.

• Section 3: Internship Program. Top-tier graduating students may be offered placements within designated government agencies (e.g., special operations, research and development) for a term of service, further strengthening the alliance.


Article IV: Restrictions and Limitations

• Section 1: Students and staff of the Athenaeum are forbidden from using their abilities on the civilian population unless under direct authorization from the Veil Council for an Article III operation.

• Section 2: The Athenaeum is prohibited from developing or researching abilities classified as "Existential Threats" (e.g., large-scale mind control, reality alteration) without direct oversight and approval from the Veil Council.

• Section 3 (The Sterling Amendment): Following the Sterling Incident, this amendment added stringent clauses about public displays of power, making any deliberate breach of the Veil an act of treason against the Accord, punishable by permanent detainment in a specialized facility.
